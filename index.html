01A LITTLE ABOUT ME

MORE THAN ONE FIGHT

Rooted in the soil.
Ready for the ring.

I'm Bilal Mohamed, an Animal Production student at Beni Suef University and a boxer with one defining dream: to become the greatest boxer in history. A world, national, or any other title would be a milestone on that long road—not the destination.

My routine starts the night before: I read a few pages before sleep, wake for Fajr prayer, then run about 10 km when time allows and skip rope for around 30 minutes. I balance college and study with strength training three days a week and boxing three days, keeping Friday for rest. When mornings are full, I run to and from the gym instead. I try to memorize half a page of Qur'an each day, keep improving my English, and learn something new. I don't get every day perfect—but I keep coming back, and I never intend to give up.

I want to build a successful life—and real financial success—not for its own sake, but so I can support people in need and orphaned children. I try to turn every setback into a lesson, learn from the people I meet, and correct one fault at a time. I'm not sure the road will be easy or even visible every day. I only know I won't stop walking it.
